**Does this look AI-generated?** No.

The interface has genuine clinical identity. The dark hero with split-layout, the PCDTs/CFM/SUS credential list in ProvaSection, and the animated pré-eclampsia case in DemoSection are all specifically grounded in Brazilian medical practice. ## Overall Impression

The brand identity is solid and genuinely distinct. The biggest missed opportunity is efficiency for the primary user: a GP under time pressure who needs to submit a case and get a result in seconds. The app has only one keyboard shortcut, the primary submit action requires a mouse click, and errors disappear silently to the console. The landing is close to shippable; the app needs one efficiency pass before it's truly respectful of a busy clinician's time.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Session list items not keyboard-accessible** — `Sidebar.jsx:153` — `<li>` has onClick but no role, tabIndex, or onKeyDown. WCAG 2.1.1/4.1.2 violation.

**[P1] "Começar grátis" routes to /login, not registration** — `Navbar.jsx:18` — primary conversion CTA sends new users to login form. Abandonment risk at highest-intent moment.

**[P1] No Ctrl+Enter for case submission** — CaseInput has no keyboard shortcut for submit. Forces mouse interaction for doctors submitting 20+ cases/day.

**[P1] PDF download failure is silent** — `Dashboard.jsx:202-213` — errors caught with console.error only, no user feedback.
